# Household Assistant SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Household Assistant is a private personal assistant messaging service available only to the owner and explicitly authorized users.

## SMS Service

Users may send SMS messages to Household Assistant to request personal assistant functions such as information, reminders, scheduling, calendar assistance, and other personal tasks.

By initiating or consenting to SMS communication with Household Assistant, users consent to receive responsive and service-related SMS messages.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on user interaction and requested tasks.

Message and data rates may apply.

## Opt-Out

Users may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages.

## Help

Users may reply HELP for assistance.

## Privacy

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Use of the service is also subject to the Household Assistant Privacy Policy.
